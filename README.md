# PSX Mall Kit Free

**15 free modular PS1-style pieces to build a corner of a supermarket** for your horror game: vinyl floors clean and dirty, a plaster ceiling, the supermarket's walls and a corner post, a 4 m opening wall with its glass entrance, sliding doors that part and SAVE-RITE's sign over them, a stocked gondola, freezer doors, a checkout whose belt runs, a register whose drawer springs out, a shopping cart and a wet floor sign. They snap to a 2 m grid. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per piece with its colliders, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Mall Kit](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-mall-kit)** has 192 pieces for the whole
supermarket and mall after closing: aisles, chillers, the deli, rotten produce, checkouts and
carts, storefronts, shutters and their key switch, escalators that run, a fountain, a food court,
a laundromat that spins, the stockroom and its breaker, working machines, a Unity package and a
level to walk and solve in your browser. Same addon, same look: it installs over this one.

For more of the same grid, [PSX Gas Station Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-gas-station-kit-free)
and [PSX Kitchen and Food Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-kitchen-and-food-free) have 15
pieces each.

### What's inside

- **Structure:** a vinyl floor clean and dirty, a plaster ceiling, the supermarket's wall and a
  corner post, the opening wall, on a 2 m grid, 3 m high.
- **The front:** a glass entrance in the opening wall, sliding doors that part, their colliders
  moving with them, and SAVE-RITE's sign over it.
- **The shop:** a stocked gondola, freezer doors, a checkout whose belt runs, a register whose
  drawer springs out, a shopping cart, a wet floor sign.
- **3 animations** on the moving parts, as clips in GLB and takes in FBX.
- **15 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, and the seamless floor, wall and ceiling
  textures as their own PNGs.
- One 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours, real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per piece: box colliders on every part (the opening's leave the way through
clear), an AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your lights fall on it, and a
gallery. Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# doors: the sliding doors in your level
doors.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the kit (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.
